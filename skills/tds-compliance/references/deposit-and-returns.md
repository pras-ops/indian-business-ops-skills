# Depositing TDS and filing the returns

> Snapshot written October 2026. Due dates and forms are set by the Income-tax Rules and change;
> confirm on https://www.incometax.gov.in and https://www.tdscpc.gov.in before filing.

## The monthly rhythm

1. **Deduct** at the earlier of credit or payment (salary: at payment).
2. **Deposit** the deducted tax by challan (ITNS-281 / e-pay tax on the income-tax portal), quoting
   the correct TAN, section, and assessment year.
   - Orientation: deposit is generally by the **7th of the following month**; the **March**
     deduction has a later date (often 30 April). Government deductors differ. Verify.
3. **Late deposit** attracts interest under **section 201(1A)** — a per-month (or part-month)
   charge that differs for "deducted late" vs "deposited late". Point to the portal/TRACES for the
   exact figure; it depends on dates.

## Quarterly returns

| Form | Covers | Deductees |
|---|---|---|
| **24Q** | TDS on **salary** (section 192) | Employees |
| **26Q** | TDS on **other resident** payments (194-series) | Vendors, landlords, contractors, etc. |
| **27Q** | TDS on payments to **non-residents** (195, etc.) | Non-resident payees |
| **27EQ** | **TCS** collected | Buyers |

- Returns are filed quarterly. Orientation due dates: Q1 ~31 Jul, Q2 ~31 Oct, Q3 ~31 Jan,
  Q4 ~31 May (verify each).
- **Late filing fee** under **section 234E** (a per-day charge until the return is filed, capped at
  the TDS amount) plus possible penalty under 271H.
- Prepare the return with the official **RPU/utility** (or approved software), validate with the
  **FVU**, and file. Each deductee row needs PAN, section, amount paid, tax deducted, and the
  challan it maps to; challan totals must tie to the deductions.

## Certificates

| Form | For | Frequency | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Form 16** | Salary (TDS under 192) | Annual | Download from TRACES after filing Q4 24Q |
| **Form 16A** | Non-salary TDS | Quarterly | Download from TRACES |
| **Form 16B** | TDS on property (194IA) | Per transaction | TRACES |
| **Form 27D** | TCS | — | TRACES |

Always issue the **TRACES-generated** certificate, not a self-made one — it carries the verified
deduction data.

## Reconciliation for the payee

The deductee sees credit only if the return is filed correctly with their **PAN**. Reconcile:

- Deductions and challans filed ↔ the payee's **Form 26AS** and **AIS/TIS**.
- A wrong PAN or a challan-mismatch shows up as a **default on TRACES** (justification report) — fix
  by filing a **correction statement**.

## Common defaults and their cause

| Default | Usual cause |
|---|---|
| Short deduction | Wrong section/rate, or PAN-error higher rate (206AA) not applied |
| Late deduction / late deposit | Timing missed → 201(1A) interest |
| Late filing fee (234E) | Return filed after the quarterly due date |
| PAN error | Invalid/typo PAN → demand; fix via correction |
