# TDS sections — matching a payment to a section

> Snapshot written October 2026 (orientation for FY 2025-26 era). Rates and thresholds are set by
> the Income-tax Act and the annual Finance Act and change most years. **Confirm every rate and
> threshold on https://www.incometax.gov.in before use.** The numbers below are indicative, to help
> identify the right section — not to file with.

## How to read this

Match the **nature of the payment** to a section, then look up the live rate and threshold. "1% / 2%"
style splits usually mean *individual/HUF vs others*. Thresholds are often *per transaction* and/or
*per year* — both can apply.

## Common resident-payment sections

| Section | Payment type | Indicative rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **192** | Salary | Slab rates | Employer estimates annual tax and deducts monthly; based on the employee's regime/declarations |
| **192A** | Premature EPF withdrawal | — | Applies above a threshold |
| **194A** | Interest other than securities | 10% | Bank/others; thresholds differ |
| **194C** | Payment to contractors / sub-contractors | 1% (ind/HUF) / 2% (others) | Threshold per contract and per year |
| **194H** | Commission or brokerage | ~2–5% (verify) | — |
| **194I** | Rent | 2% (plant/machinery) / 10% (land, building, furniture) | Threshold per year |
| **194J** | Professional / technical fees, royalty | 10%, or 2% for technical services / call-centre | Two sub-limbs with different rates |
| **194Q** | Purchase of goods | 0.1% | Buyer's turnover condition; interacts with 206C(1H) TCS — only one applies |
| **194O** | E-commerce operator paying a participant | ~1% (verify) | — |
| **194DA / 194D** | Insurance maturity / insurance commission | — | — |
| **194IA** | Transfer of immovable property | 1% | Buyer deducts; threshold on consideration |
| **194IB** | Rent by individuals/HUF not under tax audit | 5% (verify) | — |
| **194M** | Contract/professional payments by individuals not covered by 194C/194J | — | — |
| **194N** | Cash withdrawals above a limit | — | Bank deducts |

## Non-resident payments

- **Section 195** governs most payments to non-residents. Rates, surcharge and cess differ, and a
  **DTAA (tax treaty)** may reduce the rate — but claiming treaty relief needs a Tax Residency
  Certificate and often **Form 15CA / 15CB**. Do not apply a resident 194-rate here. Recommend a
  professional for cross-border payments.

## Rate uplifts to always check

- **No valid PAN (section 206AA):** deduct at a higher rate (often 20%, or the section rate if
  higher).
- **Non-filer of returns (section 206AB):** a higher rate can apply; check the payee's status on
  the income-tax compliance utility.
- **Lower/nil deduction certificate (section 197):** if the payee holds one (Form 13 approval),
  deduct at the certified lower rate.

## TCS (collected on certain sales)

- **206C(1)** — specified goods (scrap, minerals, etc.).
- **206C(1H)** — sale of goods above a turnover/consideration threshold (interacts with 194Q —
  generally only one applies to a transaction).
- **206C(1G)** — foreign remittance under LRS and overseas tour packages.
