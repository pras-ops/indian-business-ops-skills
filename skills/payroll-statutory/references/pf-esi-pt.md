# EPF, ESI and Professional Tax — mechanics

> Snapshot written October 2026. Rates, wage ceilings and coverage thresholds change and are set by
> EPFO, ESIC and each state. **Confirm on the portals below before computing a real payroll.** The
> indicative figures are to explain the mechanism, not to file with.

## EPF (Employees' Provident Fund)

- **Authority / portal:** EPFO — https://www.epfindia.gov.in (employer unified portal).
- **Coverage:** mandatory once an establishment crosses the headcount threshold (commonly 20
  employees — verify). Smaller firms can register voluntarily.
- **Contribution (indicative):** employee ~12% of PF wages; employer ~12%, of which a portion goes
  to the Pension Scheme (EPS) and the rest to PF. A wage ceiling may cap the mandatory base
  (commonly ₹15,000/month — verify, and note some employers contribute on actual higher wages).
- **PF wages** are generally basic + dearness allowance (and certain allowances per court rulings).
  How the structure splits basic vs allowances therefore changes the PF liability.
- **Each employee** needs a **UAN** (Universal Account Number); link the member and KYC.
- **Monthly filing:** the **ECR** (Electronic Challan-cum-Return) — upload member-wise wages and
  contributions, generate the challan, pay. Orientation due date around the **15th** of the
  following month (verify).

## ESI (Employees' State Insurance)

- **Authority / portal:** ESIC — https://www.esic.gov.in.
- **Coverage:** establishments above a headcount threshold (commonly 10 — verify), for employees
  earning at or below a **wage ceiling** (commonly ₹21,000/month, higher for persons with
  disability — verify). Employees above the ceiling are out of ESI.
- **Contribution (indicative):** employee ~0.75% and employer ~3.25% of gross wages (verify current
  rates).
- **Contribution periods:** ESI runs on two fixed half-yearly contribution periods; an employee who
  is covered at the start of a period generally stays covered through it even if wages later cross
  the ceiling.
- **Monthly filing:** contribution filed on the ESIC portal; orientation due date around the
  **15th** of the following month (verify).

## Professional Tax (PT) — state-specific

- **Authority:** the **state** commercial-tax / PT department — there is no central PT. States that
  levy it include Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, Tamil
  Nadu, Madhya Pradesh and others; several states (e.g. Delhi, Haryana, UP) do **not** levy PT.
- **What varies by state:** whether PT exists at all, the salary **slabs** and amounts, the
  **periodicity** (monthly/annually), employer **registration (PTRC)** vs employee **enrolment
  (PTEC)**, and due dates. A common feature is a statutory **annual cap** per person.
- **Always ask which state** and confirm that state's current schedule before deducting.

## Labour Welfare Fund (LWF) — state-specific

- A small contribution in some states, usually periodic (half-yearly/annual), split between
  employee and employer. Check the specific state.

## Reconciliation

Each month, the sum of the deductions on payslips should equal what's deposited:

- Employee PF + employer PF → ECR challan.
- Employee + employer ESI → ESI challan.
- PT deducted → state PT payment.
- TDS on salary → income-tax challan (see `tds-compliance`).

A mismatch usually means an arrear, a mid-month joiner/leaver, or a ceiling crossed.
