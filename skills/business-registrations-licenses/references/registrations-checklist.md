# Registrations and licences — who issues what

> Snapshot written October 2026. Thresholds, fees and processes change; several items are
> state-specific. **Confirm on each official portal below before relying on a threshold or fee.**

## Core registrations

| Registration | Issued by / portal | When mandatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PAN** (entity) | Income Tax (NSDL/UTIITSL) | Always for a business entity | Needed for bank account, GST, TDS |
| **TAN** | Income Tax | If the business deducts TDS/TCS | Quote on all TDS challans/returns |
| **Company incorporation** | MCA — **SPICe+** | For Pvt Ltd / OPC / Public | Bundles DIN, PAN, TAN, EPFO, ESIC, PT (some states), bank account |
| **LLP incorporation** | MCA — **FiLLiP** | For an LLP | Then LLP agreement filing |
| **GST registration (GSTIN)** | GST portal — https://www.gst.gov.in | Above turnover limit; inter-state supply; e-commerce sellers; RCM; casual/non-resident | Several independent triggers — check all |
| **Udyam (MSME)** | https://udyamregistration.gov.in | Optional but recommended | Free; self-declared; unlocks MSME benefits and delayed-payment protection |
| **Shops & Establishments** | **State** labour department | Most commercial premises | State-specific name, fee, renewal |
| **Professional Tax (PTEC/PTRC)** | **State** commercial-tax dept | Where PT applies | See `payroll-statutory` |
| **Bank current account** | Bank | To operate | Uses the above as KYC |
| **EPF / ESI registration** | EPFO / ESIC | Once headcount thresholds crossed | See `payroll-statutory` |

## Activity-specific licences

| Licence | Issued by / portal | For |
|---|---|---|
| **FSSAI** (Basic / State / Central) | https://www.fssai.gov.in (FoSCoS) | Any food business; tier by scale |
| **Importer-Exporter Code (IEC)** | DGFT — https://www.dgft.gov.in | Importing or exporting goods/services |
| **GST LUT** | GST portal | Exporting without paying IGST (letter of undertaking) |
| **Trade licence** | Local **municipal** body | Many premises-based trades |
| **Pollution Control consent** | State Pollution Control Board | Manufacturing / specified activities |
| **Drug licence, etc.** | Sector regulator | Regulated goods/services |
| **Professional council / RBI / SEBI** | Respective regulator | Regulated professions and finance |

## Typical documents to assemble

- Identity/address proof of proprietor/partners/directors (PAN, Aadhaar, passport-size photos).
- Proof of business premises (rent agreement + owner NOC, or ownership proof, utility bill).
- Entity proof (incorporation certificate / partnership deed / LLP agreement).
- Bank details (cancelled cheque / statement).
- Digital Signature (DSC) and DIN/DPIN for company/LLP filings.

## A sensible order for a new business

1. Decide structure → incorporate (if company/LLP) → get **PAN** and **TAN**.
2. **GST registration** if any trigger applies.
3. **Udyam** (quick win).
4. **Shops & Establishments** (state) and **Professional Tax** registration (state, if applicable).
5. **Activity licences** (FSSAI / IEC / trade licence / sector permit) as needed.
6. **Bank current account.**
7. **EPF / ESI** registration when you cross the hiring thresholds.
