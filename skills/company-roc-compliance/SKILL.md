# Company / LLP ROC compliance (India)

Every company and LLP registered with the MCA carries recurring obligations under the Companies Act
2013 (or the LLP Act), independent of tax. Missing them attracts per-day penalties and can
disqualify directors or strike off the company. This skill helps a business know **what it must
file, when, and in what order** — it does not file for the user or give a legal opinion.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Forms, thresholds, fees and due dates are set by MCA rules and change (the MCA portal moved to
> the V3 system; some form names and processes changed). Never assert a current form or date from
> memory. Confirm on https://www.mca.gov.in. The reference file holds dated snapshots. For
> anything contested — a strike-off, a compounding, a notice — recommend a Company Secretary (CS).
---

## First, identify the entity

The obligations differ sharply by type, so establish this before anything:

- **Private Limited (Pvt Ltd)** / **Public Limited** — full Companies Act regime.
- **One Person Company (OPC)** — lighter (e.g. MGT-7A, relaxed meeting rules).
- **LLP** — LLP Act regime: **Form 11** (annual return) and **Form 8** (statement of account &
  solvency), different dates, and audit only above turnover/contribution limits.
- **Section 8 (non-profit)** — extra conditions.

Also note: date of incorporation (first-year filings differ), financial year, paid-up capital,
turnover, and number of directors/members.

## The annual cycle

Read [`references/annual-filings.md`](references/annual-filings.md) for the form-by-form map and
where to confirm current due dates. The shape for a typical Pvt Ltd:

1. **Finalise accounts** → get them audited (a company audit is mandatory regardless of turnover).
2. **Board meeting** to approve the financials and the Director's Report.
3. **Annual General Meeting (AGM)** — hold within the statutory window after year-end.
4. **File AOC-4** (financial statements) with the ROC after the AGM.
5. **File MGT-7 / MGT-7A** (annual return) with the ROC.
6. **DIR-3 KYC** for every director with a DIN — an annual personal compliance, separate from the
   company filings.
7. Earlier one-offs each year as applicable: **ADT-1** (auditor appointment/ratification),
   **DPT-3** (return of deposits/loans), **MSME-1** (dues to MSME suppliers), etc.

## Ongoing, not just annual

- **Board meetings:** minimum number per year with maximum gaps between them (OPC/small companies
  relaxed). Keep **minutes**.
- **Statutory registers:** members, directors, charges, related-party contracts, etc. — maintained
  and kept current, not reconstructed at year-end.
- **Event-based filings** whenever something changes: director appointment/resignation (**DIR-12**),
  increase in capital or allotment (**PAS-3**, **SH-7**), charge creation/satisfaction
  (**CHG-1/CHG-4**), change of registered office (**INC-22**), etc. These are time-bound from the
  event date.

## How to help, concretely

- **"What do we file this year?"** — build a checklist for the specific entity type with the order
  above, each item marked "confirm current due date on MCA", and flag the first-year differences if
  newly incorporated.
- **Prepare supporting documents** — draft board/AGM minute structures, the Director's Report
  skeleton, and the data each form needs (directors, shareholding, financials), for a professional
  to review and certify.
- **A missed-filing situation** — explain the additional-fee mechanism (per-day additional fees
  that escalate), the risk (director disqualification, strike-off), and the route back
  (file with additional fee, or compounding) — then recommend a CS.

## Guardrails

- Many MCA forms require certification by a practising CA/CS/CMA with a **digital signature (DSC)**
  — the skill prepares and organises; it cannot certify or file.
- Keep company vs LLP straight; applying company forms to an LLP (or vice versa) is a common error.
- Mark every form name, fee and date "verify on MCA"; the V3 migration changed several.
