# MCA / ROC annual and event-based filings

> Snapshot written October 2026. Form names, timelines and fees are set by MCA rules and have
> changed with the V3 portal migration. **Confirm on https://www.mca.gov.in before relying on any
> form or date.** Indicative timelines are for orientation.

## Company (Pvt Ltd / Public) — annual

| Form | Purpose | Indicative timing |
|---|---|---|
| **AOC-4** | File audited financial statements | Within 30 days of the AGM |
| **MGT-7** | Annual return (share capital, directors, shareholding) | Within 60 days of the AGM |
| **MGT-7A** | Abridged annual return for **OPC and small companies** | Within 60 days of the AGM |
| **ADT-1** | Intimate auditor appointment | Within 15 days of the AGM that appoints them |
| **DIR-3 KYC** | Annual KYC of every director with a DIN | Typically by 30 September |
| **DPT-3** | Return of deposits / amounts not treated as deposits | Annual (orientation: by 30 June) |
| **MSME-1** | Half-yearly return of outstanding dues to MSME suppliers | Half-yearly |

Meetings that precede the filings:

- **AGM:** first AGM within 9 months of the first financial year-end; later AGMs within 6 months of
  year-end (max 15 months between AGMs). Verify.
- **Board meetings:** minimum number per year with a maximum gap; minutes maintained.

## LLP — annual

| Form | Purpose | Indicative timing |
|---|---|---|
| **Form 11** | Annual return of the LLP | Within 60 days of year-end (orientation: by 30 May) |
| **Form 8** | Statement of Account & Solvency | Orientation: by 30 October |
| **DIR-3 KYC** | KYC for designated partners with a DIN/DPIN | Annual |

LLP **audit** is required only above turnover / contribution limits — verify the current limits.

## Event-based filings (time-bound from the event)

| Event | Form |
|---|---|
| Appointment / resignation / change of director | **DIR-12** |
| Allotment of shares | **PAS-3** |
| Increase in authorised capital / alteration | **SH-7** |
| Creation / modification of charge | **CHG-1** |
| Satisfaction of charge | **CHG-4** |
| Change of registered office | **INC-22** (and INC-23/INC-28 where approval needed) |
| Change of company name | **INC-24** (after name approval) |

These carry short deadlines from the event date; late filing adds per-day fees.

## Consequences of missing filings

- **Additional fees** accrue per day and escalate with delay (can be a multiple of the normal fee).
- Prolonged default can lead to **director disqualification** (section 164) and company
  **strike-off**.
- The route back is usually: file with additional fee, or **compounding** of the offence — handle
  with a practising CS.

## Certification and signing

Most MCA forms are filed on the **V3 portal** and signed with a **DSC**; several require
certification by a practising **CA / CS / CMA**. Prepare and organise the data; a professional
certifies and files.
