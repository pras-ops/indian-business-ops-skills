# Disclaimer

This repository is a set of **operational aids** for working with Indian business compliance using
an AI agent. It is published for general information and workflow automation only.

**It is not professional advice.** Nothing here is legal, tax, accounting or financial advice, and
using these skills does not create any professional relationship. Tax and company law in India
change frequently and often apply differently depending on your entity type, turnover, state and
industry.

**Verify before you act.** Every rate, threshold, due date and form reference must be confirmed
against the current official source before you file anything or make a decision:

- GST — Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs: https://www.cbic-gst.gov.in and the GST portal https://www.gst.gov.in
- Income tax and TDS/TCS — Income Tax Department: https://www.incometax.gov.in
- Company / LLP (ROC) — Ministry of Corporate Affairs: https://www.mca.gov.in
- EPF — https://www.epfindia.gov.in | ESI — https://www.esic.gov.in
- MSME / Udyam — https://udyamregistration.gov.in
- Foreign trade (IEC) — https://www.dgft.gov.in | FSSAI — https://www.fssai.gov.in

**Engage a professional** — a Chartered Accountant (CA), Company Secretary (CS), Cost Accountant or
advocate — for filings, assessments, notices, and any material decision. The authors and
contributors accept no liability for any loss, penalty or interest arising from use of this
material.

**Transitions in progress (as of October 2026).** Two large changes may have moved things since
these files were written, so treat anything specific as needing confirmation:

- A new **Income-tax Act, 2025** is reported to replace the Income-tax Act, 1961 and **renumbers
  sections**. The TDS skill refers to sections by their long-standing numbers (192, 194C, 194J,
  195, …) — these may now be different. Confirm the current section on https://www.incometax.gov.in.
- India's **four Labour Codes** (Wages; Industrial Relations; Social Security; Occupational Safety)
  are being brought into force and can change the statutory definition of *wages*, which feeds
  **PF, ESI, gratuity and bonus**. Confirm current rules on https://www.epfindia.gov.in,
  https://www.esic.gov.in and the relevant code before computing payroll.

**Snapshots.** Where a reference file states a specific number, it is labelled with the date it was
written and is a snapshot for orientation, not a live figure.
